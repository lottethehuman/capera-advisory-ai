Experience

A fractional operating partner that builds the AI, not an AI vendor that has never run a business.

Charlotte Chen Pienaar, Founder & Principal of Capera Advisory, studied Economics at New York University before building a 15-year career across finance (KPMG, Macquarie Capital), technology (500 Startups alumna) and operating roles. She has run businesses as a founder, fractional CEO and fractional COO, so she has sat in her clients' seat and hit the same bottlenecks they face.

Work to date spans hospitality, leisure, manufacturing and consumer brands.

Fractional COO (Multi-Location Business)
Ran stakeholder interviews and delivered a due-diligence report mapping the highest-value opportunities. Surfaced the metrics that matter most, then built a command center with BI dashboards tracking the financial and operational numbers that give the daily pulse of the business. The dashboards pull both historical and live data, so leadership can spot patterns and look ahead, not just react. Added tools for the team's daily rhythm, so decisions run on numbers instead of feel. Automated workflows that had been running manually in Excel and WhatsApp. Secure login and role-based access protect the platform.
Fractional CEO (Physical Venue Turnaround)
Rescued a project in crisis within 12 weeks. Phase one: led crisis management, designed the strategy for a full operational rehaul, set OKRs, managed vendors, built a project dashboard to track the physical rebuild, and took branding and website from unclear direction to a clear brief through to launch. Phase two: stood up an AI-powered operating system across sales, marketing, HR, legal, finance and ops, with SOPs and workflows automating day-to-day operations and board-level BI. Hired a general manager, coached him on process and facilitated his success in building a full in-house team to run the platform independently.
Fractional COO (B2B + B2C Business)
Worked with the founder to define the strategic direction for both the B2B and B2C units, then translated that into OKRs and clear metrics the leadership team could run against. Built one operating layer across six departments, replacing information silos with shared visibility. Board reporting pulls straight from that layer. Trained the team on new tools and workflows to drive adoption. A shared knowledge base and new processes keep the team scaling without adding headcount.
Founder / CEO (Consumer Brand)
Founded and ran an international consumer brand as a solo founder, selling across wholesale and DTC in the US market without a local presence. Front of house: closed and managed B2B accounts and ran B2C sales and marketing programs remotely, with a paid ads system and reporting dashboard for live performance visibility. Back of house: managed complex multi-vendor product development, supply chain and procurement supporting both B2B and B2C, and set up systems across finance, legal, HR, ops and shareholder management. One operating layer and command center gave the remote team a single source of truth, shared direction and clarity on priorities. A lean team with the output of one twice its size.
