Beauty (Founder-Operator)
Founded and scaled a US beauty brand from $0 to seven figures across wholesale and DTC, building an operating system to orchestrate a 100% remote team across 7 departments — product development, supply chain, design, sales, brand marketing, performance marketing, and finance — running a lean team, with output like a headcount twice its size.

Rescued a project from crisis to fully operating in 12 weeks — resetting direction with OKRs, managing the physical rebuild through one project management system, and standing up every back-end department (HR, finance, legal, ops) with AI, then defining SOPs and workflows to automate day-to-day operations and building board-level BI, now run daily by a full in-house team.

Built a custom operating layer for a founder-led business spanning B2B and B2C across six departments — fixing information silos that had built up across the team, giving the founder clarity to make decisions across both units, and designing new workflows and a knowledge base built to scale with AI, without adding new hires.

Interviewed stakeholders across the business to find where workflows were breaking down, redesigned them for efficiency and automation, and built a live command center — BI dashboards by department turning scattered sales, inventory, POS, opex, and labor data into real-time decisions.
