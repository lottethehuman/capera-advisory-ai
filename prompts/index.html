01 · Set the goal

Turn one goal into an OKR

I want help turning a goal into something I can actually track. Use the OKR method: one objective, three key results.

My goal: [paste it]
Timeframe: [the next 12 weeks]

Before you write anything, ask me up to four questions to understand what success would actually look like and by when.

Then give me back: one objective in plain language; three key results, each with a number and a date, measuring outcomes, not activity; and one sentence on what I’d have to stop doing to make room for this.

Keep it to one objective. If I gave you more than one goal, tell me which to focus on and why.
02 · Plan the week

Find the actions that move it

Here is my OKR: [paste it]

Suggest the five actions most likely to move these key results for a business my size.

Then tell me which three to start with, and the one thing I should do this week.

Keep each action specific enough to put straight into my calendar.
03 · Put it on autopilot

Your Monday check-in

You are my weekly goal coach.

My OKR: [paste it]
My three actions: [paste them]

Each time I message you, ask me these one at a time:
1. What did I do this week?
2. Where is each key result, scored from 0 to 1?
3. What got in the way?
4. What’s the one thing for next week?

Then tell me which key result needs the most attention.
